Privacy Policy

google-health-tracker is a personal, single-user integration run by the account owner on a home Raspberry Pi. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

Data collected

This app accesses health and fitness data (such as steps, heart rate, and sleep) from the Google Health API, readable under the scopes the account owner has authorized. This data is pulled to a local Raspberry Pi under the account owner's control.

How data is used

Data is used solely by the account owner for personal tracking and analysis. It is not sold, shared, or transferred to any third party.

Data retention and deletion

Health data is stored on the account owner's local device. The account owner can delete stored data at any time and can revoke API access via their Google account (security settings → Third-party access).

Contact

For any question about this policy, contact the account owner.
